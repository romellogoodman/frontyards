Frontyards — Websites artists own

Instagram doesn't care about your art. No platform does — your work is content to them, feed material, cropped to their grid and buried by their algorithm the moment you stop feeding it. And you don't own any of it: the account, the audience, the archive can be throttled, redesigned, or deleted out from under you. Your work deserves a place that exists only because your work exists. Frontyard builds websites artists own — a permanent address for your practice, designed around the work itself, that nobody can take down but you.

The cost is simple, and it's not really money. You trade me a piece of your art — that's the fee. I choose the artists I work with the way a collector chooses work, and the trade is the point: your site joins my practice, your piece joins my collection. After that it's $100 a year for maintenance, and that's it. No packages, no upsells, no invoice surprises. Once a year we renew, I make sure everything still works, and your art stays exactly where it belongs: at your own address, in your own front yard.

— Romello
